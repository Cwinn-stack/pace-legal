Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · DRAFT, not yet reviewed by a lawyer

This policy explains what the Pace app ("we", "the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we use your location only to tell whether you went to your gym, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email those services share with us.
  • Profile: the name, bio and photo you choose to add.
  • Your gym: the gym you pick, including its address and map coordinates.
  • Location: the app asks iOS to tell it when your phone enters or leaves the area around your gym. It does not track where you go otherwise. Entry and exit times are kept on your phone to work out whether a visit lasted long enough; only the finished result (the date of a counted visit) is sent to our servers.
  • Goals and visits: the goals you set, your stake amount, the dates of counted visits, your time zone, and whether each goal was hit or missed.
  • Payments: card details are entered into and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number. We keep the card brand and last four digits (for example "Visa •••• 4242"), your Stripe customer ID, and a record of each charge.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device, so we can send reminders.

Why we use it

  • To count gym visits automatically and decide whether you met your goal.
  • To charge your stake when you miss a goal, and to process repeal purchases.
  • To send reminders, such as when you must go today to stay on track.
  • To show your profile, streaks and milestones in the app.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who we share it with

Only the services the app needs in order to work:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Stripe stores your card and processes charges.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Google powers gym search (Google Places) and, if you choose it, Google sign-in.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, if you choose it.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, gym, goals, visits, notification token and saved card are deleted. Stripe keeps its own records of past charges, as payment law requires.

Your choices

  • Location and notifications can be turned off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without "Always" location access, visits can't be counted automatically.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete Account. If a goal is running or a charge is unpaid, you'll need to finish or settle it first.
  • Questions or requests about your data: email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
